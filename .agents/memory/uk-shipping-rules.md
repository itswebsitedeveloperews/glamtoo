---
name: UK shipping rules
description: The agreed delivery pricing, eligibility, and messaging for Glam Too.
---

For UK orders, standard delivery costs £3.95 when the item subtotal is below £75 and is free from £75 inclusive. Express delivery costs £6.95 at every order value. Standard delivery takes 2–4 working days and express delivery takes 1–2 working days.

**Why:** Customer-facing delivery copy, cart totals, checkout totals, and payment calculations had drifted apart.

**How to apply:** Determine the standard-delivery threshold from the pre-discount item subtotal, calculate the fee server-side for the Stripe amount, and keep all storefront messages aligned with those rules.

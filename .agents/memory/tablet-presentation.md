---
name: Tablet storefront presentation
description: Merchant's responsive storefront requests for grids, centered cards and product headings above galleries
---

Homepage collection tiles and product cards, Shop grid cards, related products, and category-page product cards
(including Clothes), should use half widths on iPad/tablet, with two per row.
Category banner headings should be smaller on iPad/tablet.
All account-page H1 headings should be smaller on iPad/tablet and mobile,
without changing desktop heading sizes.
Account-section H1 headings should capitalize the first letter of each word,
for example “Your Addresses.”, at every screen size.
Center an unpaired final homepage category tile or product card on iPad, keeping its half width.
Let homepage category-tile text use the available tile width on iPad rather
than a narrow text column, while preserving the two-column tiles.
On product pages, put the category and product title above the gallery on iPad
and mobile, preserving the desktop layout.
Keep every product-detail gallery smaller and left-aligned on iPad, using a portrait
frame; do not apply this image sizing to phones or desktops.
For grid and image sizing, keep phone and desktop presentation unchanged.
Show a vertical, small gallery-thumbnail rail to the left of the main image on
iPad, like desktop. Keep the horizontal thumbnail strip below the image on mobile.
Related products should be a horizontally swipeable card slider on mobile only,
with part of the next card visible; preserve the iPad and desktop product grids.
Let the mobile Related Products slider reach the right screen edge, while
preserving its left inset and card widths.
Apply the same right-edge treatment to the mobile Our Current Obsessions slider,
without changing its left inset or card widths.
Use the same mobile-only card slider for the homepage Our Current Obsessions
section; keep its iPad grid and centered unpaired card, and desktop grid unchanged.
The Shop page's mobile card view should show two equal-width products per row.
Keep its single-column List view and its iPad/desktop layouts unchanged.
The homepage Shop by Moment / Outfits for Every Plan section should also use
the same mobile-only swipeable card slider, reaching the right screen edge.
Preserve its pink background, overlaid copy, and iPad/desktop layouts.
Use smaller, 20px product titles in the mobile Shop by Moment slider, keeping
descriptions, links, and tablet/desktop typography unchanged.
FAQ answer text should be 14px on mobile only; keep question text and
tablet/desktop answer typography unchanged.
The FAQ page's Delivery table text should be 12px on mobile only, without
changing the 14px FAQ answers or tablet/desktop table typography.

**Why:** The merchant explicitly requested two-column collections and products,
then a smaller “New Arrivals” category heading and two-column Clothes category
products, using iPad Mini portrait references. The merchant also requested
centering the final tile or product card when it sits alone in a homepage grid.
The merchant requested moving the product heading above its image on iPad and mobile.
The merchant supplied a smaller portrait image reference for iPad product pages.
The merchant subsequently requested left alignment of the iPad product gallery,
superseding the earlier centered-gallery presentation while preserving photo size.
The merchant reiterated that left alignment applies to all product-page sliders,
not just the Military Style Jacket example.
The merchant explicitly requested half-width related products on iPad.
The merchant requested smaller H1 headings across account sections on iPad.
The merchant subsequently requested smaller account-page headings on mobile too.
The merchant requested wider text areas inside homepage category tiles on iPad.
The merchant requested first-letter capitalization for account headings.
The merchant explicitly requested half-width Shop page product cards on iPad.
The merchant reported that the gallery slider was missing on iPad and mobile
and explicitly requested adding it there.
The merchant subsequently requested desktop-style vertical thumbnails on iPad
and explicitly confirmed the horizontal mobile slider was correct as is.
The merchant requested a reference-style Related Products slider on mobile only.
The merchant then explicitly requested removing that slider's right-side space.
The merchant also requested removing the right-side space from the homepage slider.
The merchant then requested the same mobile slider for Our Current Obsessions.
The merchant requested 50/50-width Shop product listings on mobile, using a
two-column reference.
The merchant requested the Related Products slider treatment for Shop by Moment
on mobile too, explicitly without right-side space.
The merchant then highlighted the overlaid product titles and requested a smaller font.
The merchant explicitly requested 14px FAQ answers on mobile.
The merchant separately highlighted the FAQ Delivery table and requested 12px
on mobile.

**How to apply:** Scope these adjustments to tablet layouts, not global grid or
heading rules. Do not apply the desktop collection collage's spanning tile to
the tablet layout. Keep category heading semantics and body-copy sizes unchanged.

Portrait promotional cards in narrow slider tracks need enough height for their
overlaid copy and an explicitly track-constrained width.

**Why:** Long moment-card headings exceeded the shortest narrow-phone frames.
Increasing the minimum height alone let the aspect ratio widen cards beyond
their tracks, causing overlap.

**How to apply:** Preserve readable copy, constrain cards to their track width,
and check long titles at the narrowest supported phone size as well as edge reach.

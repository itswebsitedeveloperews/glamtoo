---
name: Tablet storefront presentation
description: Merchant's responsive storefront requests for grids, centered cards and product headings above galleries
---

Homepage collection tiles and product cards, and category-page product cards
(including Clothes), should use half widths on iPad/tablet, with two per row.
Category banner headings should be smaller on iPad/tablet.
Center an unpaired final homepage category tile or product card on iPad, keeping its half width.
On product pages, put the category and product title above the gallery on iPad
and mobile, preserving the desktop layout.
Keep the main product photo smaller and centered on iPad, using a portrait
frame; do not apply this image sizing to phones or desktops.
Keep phone and desktop presentation unchanged.

**Why:** The merchant explicitly requested two-column collections and products,
then a smaller “New Arrivals” category heading and two-column Clothes category
products, using iPad Mini portrait references. The merchant also requested
centering the final tile or product card when it sits alone in a homepage grid.
The merchant requested moving the product heading above its image on iPad and mobile.
The merchant supplied a smaller portrait image reference for iPad product pages.

**How to apply:** Scope these adjustments to tablet layouts, not global grid or
heading rules. Do not apply the desktop collection collage's spanning tile to
the tablet layout. Keep category heading semantics and body-copy sizes unchanged.

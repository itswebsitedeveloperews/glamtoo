---
name: Announcement copy ownership
description: Why a new announcement bar starts empty rather than with sample promotions.
---

Leave announcements empty until the merchant supplies or saves approved copy. Do not seed inferred shipping promises, discounts, or sample offers.

**Why:** The announcement feature request supplied behaviour but no approved promotional copy. Invented offers could mislead customers.

**How to apply:** An empty, invisible announcement bar is an intentional valid state, not missing seed data. Test with temporary isolated fixtures and remove them afterward.

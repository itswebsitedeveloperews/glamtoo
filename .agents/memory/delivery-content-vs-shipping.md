Product-page delivery advice and checkout must use one editable source of rates and promises. Shipping manages that source; the Delivery tab is a preview, not a second pricing editor.

**Why:** The merchant explicitly approved the product-page advice over the conflicting checkout settings: UK Saturday £8.99, UK Standard £3.99 with 3–7 working days, and UK Next Day £5.99 with its existing cutoff/restriction details. This supersedes the earlier decision to keep product delivery information independent.

**How to apply:** Changes to names, rates, descriptions, enabled services, or free thresholds must be reflected in both product advice and checkout. Preserve existing shipping identifiers and thresholds when adopting advice; do not silently reinterpret saved selections or overwrite future merchant edits with retired delivery-table values. Payment amounts remain server-resolved.
